The Core Problem: Confidence vs. Factual Correctness

In high-stakes business environments, language models generate plausible yet factually incorrect SQL statements with identical linguistic confidence. This repository implements the two fundamental pillars of trustworthy generative AI systems:

  • Dynamic Routing: Selecting the most cost-effective model capable of handling a request's specific complexity level (e.g., lightweight models for simple timestamp filters, frontier models for multi-table join and date logic).
  • Majority Voting Consensus: Querying heterogeneous models in parallel and accepting answers only when a k-of-n quorum is reached, with transparent abstention when models diverge.
