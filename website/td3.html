TD 3: Cost-Performance Optimization & Live Demonstration

Synthesizing accuracy, latency, and token economics into an optimal production architecture: evaluating the trade-off between the cost of verification and the business cost of an unchecked model error.

Empirical Pricing Baseline from TD 2

TD 2 established the official model portfolio token pricing (e.g. openai/gpt-oss-120b#openrouter at $0.15 in / $0.60 out per M tokens = $0.000711 USD per request, alongside zero cash spend for free routes) and proved that no free model is sufficient on its own for complex age logic. This establishes the exact “Price of Checking” for TD 3.

1. The Economic Trade-off: Cost of Verification vs. Cost of Error

Direct Cost

Token Expenditure

Sending a prompt with full relational schema and reasoning instructions consumes between 400 and 1,200 tokens per call. At high volume, multi-model panels multiply token consumption by the panel size n.

Risk-Weighted Cost

Business Cost of an Error

An erroneous revenue figure in an ERP or accounting system can result in tax penalties, misplaced inventory orders, or flawed financial disclosures. Investing in verification is insurance against bad model draws.

2. Production Configuration Benchmarking (5 Production Profiles)

In accordance with the roadmap defined in TD 2 Section 7, five production configurations are modeled across the 20 benchmark questions:

Configuration Architecture Description Empirical Reliability Relative Cash Cost Operational Recommendation
Config 1: Cheapest Single Model Lowest-cost capable model per family (e.g. GPT-20B or Qwen). Medium (~75-90%) $0.00 (Free Quota) Internal dashboards, non-critical ad-hoc exploration.
Config 2: Best Single Frontier Direct dispatch to openai/gpt-oss-120b. Flawless (100% in TD 1) Moderate ($0.000711/req) Ad-hoc analyst queries where reasoning depth is required.
Config 3: Fixed 2-of-3 Panel Majority voting across 3 models (k=2). High (80%), but 5% False Consensus (C09) Low ($0.00 cash) Unsuitable for customer/payout figures without sanity checks.
Config 4: Full 3-of-5 Quorum Majority voting across all 5 models (k=3). 100% Decision Precision (0% False Consensus) Optimal ($0.00 cash) Audited reporting, high-integrity data validation.
Config 5: Subgroup Adaptive Router Dynamic rule routing easy queries to compact models, hard to frontier, with failover & abstention. Optimal (~98-100%) Lowest Effective Cost Production enterprise workflows, customer-facing and payouts.
