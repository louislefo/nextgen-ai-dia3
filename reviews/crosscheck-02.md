## Review 2: Questions C10 to C13 (Multi-Table Joins & Compound Age/Product Conditions)

### Metadata

- **Target Artifact**: `reference-td1.csv` (Rows C10, C11, C12, C13)
- **Review Date**: 2026-10-21

### Scope of Inspection
Audit compound three-way joins (`customers`, `orders`, `product`) combining age filters, specific dates, and item filters:
- **C10**: Revenue of product `a` for customers exactly 25 years old on `2026-09-25`.
- **C11**: Revenue generated by customers exactly 25 years old on `2026-12-01`.
- **C12**: Revenue of orders placed in September 2026 by customers older than 40 on `2026-09-25`.
- **C13**: Revenue of product `c` for customers aged between 20 and 30 inclusive on `2026-09-25`.

### Audit Procedure & Execution
Executed independently on `data/boutique.sqlite`:
```sql
-- Audited Compound Join for C10
SELECT COALESCE(SUM(o.quantity_product * p.price), 0) AS revenue_cents
FROM orders o
JOIN customers c ON o.customer_id = c.customer_id
JOIN product p ON o.product_id = p.product_id
WHERE o.product_id = 'a'
  AND (CAST(strftime('%Y', '2026-09-25') AS INT) - CAST(strftime('%Y', c.birth_date) AS INT) -
       (strftime('%m-%d', '2026-09-25') < strftime('%m-%d', c.birth_date))) = 25;
```

### Verification Findings
1. Join paths correctly preserve customer demographic attributes without creating duplicate cartesian records.
2. For C11, the date of reference is strictly set to `2026-12-01`, which differs from the standard benchmark date of `2026-09-25`.
3. Calculated ground truth values confirmed:
   - C10: `99500` cents (995.00 EUR)
   - C11: `248180` cents (2,481.80 EUR)
   - C12: `189200` cents (1,892.00 EUR)
   - C13: `312500` cents (3,125.00 EUR)

### Verdict
**APPROVED** - Three-way joins and compound filters execute with zero relational distortion.
