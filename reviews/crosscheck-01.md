## Review 1: Questions C01 to C03 (Date Periods & Half-Open Intervals)

### Metadata

- **Target Artifact**: `reference-td1.csv` (Rows C01, C02, C03)
- **Review Date**: 2026-10-21

### Scope of Inspection
Audit the deterministic SQL implementation of date boundaries for half-open intervals `[start, end)`:
- **C01**: Revenue excluding VAT in euro cents for orders placed from `2026-01-01 00:00:00` to `2026-02-01 00:00:00`.
- **C02**: Revenue excluding VAT in euro cents for orders placed from `2026-02-01 00:00:00` to `2026-03-01 00:00:00`.
- **C03**: Revenue excluding VAT in euro cents for orders placed from `2026-03-01 00:00:00` to `2026-04-01 00:00:00`.

### Audit Procedure & Execution
Executed independently on `data/boutique.sqlite`:
```sql
-- Audited Query for C01
SELECT COALESCE(SUM(o.quantity_product * p.price), 0) AS revenue_cents
FROM orders o
JOIN product p ON o.product_id = p.product_id
WHERE o.date >= '2026-01-01 00:00:00' AND o.date < '2026-02-01 00:00:00';
```

### Verification Findings
1. Half-open interval convention (`>= 'start'` and `< 'end'`) is strictly respected across all three queries, preventing double-counting of midnight edge orders.
2. Join condition `o.product_id = p.product_id` correctly resolves unit pricing in euro cents.
3. Default `COALESCE(..., 0)` guarantees non-null integer return.
4. Calculated ground truth values confirmed:
   - C01: `341850` cents (3,418.50 EUR)
   - C02: `389330` cents (3,893.30 EUR)
   - C03: `378390` cents (3,783.90 EUR)

### Verdict
**APPROVED** - Query semantics are flawless and values match instructor answer keys.

## Review 2: Questions C04 to C06 (Customer Age Calculations & Order-Date Age)

### Metadata

- **Auditor / Reviewer**: Louis Le Forestier
- **Target Artifact**: `reference-td1.csv` (Rows C04, C05, C06)
- **Review Date**: 2026-10-21

### Scope of Inspection
Audit the customer completed age arithmetic in SQLite against edge-case birthdates:
- **C04**: Revenue generated by customers exactly 25 years old on `2026-09-25`.
- **C05**: Revenue generated by customers strictly older than 30 on `2026-09-25`.
- **C06**: Revenue of orders placed in 2026 by customers who were exactly 25 years old on the order date.

### Audit Procedure & Execution
Executed independently on `data/boutique.sqlite`:
```sql
-- Audited Age Logic for C04
SELECT COALESCE(SUM(o.quantity_product * p.price), 0) AS revenue_cents
FROM orders o
JOIN customers c ON o.customer_id = c.customer_id
JOIN product p ON o.product_id = p.product_id
WHERE c.birth_date IS NOT NULL
  AND (CAST(strftime('%Y', '2026-09-25') AS INT) - CAST(strftime('%Y', c.birth_date) AS INT) -
       (strftime('%m-%d', '2026-09-25') < strftime('%m-%d', c.birth_date))) = 25;
```

### Verification Findings
1. The completed age expression handles leap years and birthday anniversaries accurately without naive year subtraction.
2. For C06, the reference date dynamically binds to `o.date` rather than a static benchmark date.
3. Calculated ground truth values confirmed:
   - C04: `258180` cents (2,581.80 EUR)
   - C05: `1054320` cents (10,543.20 EUR)
   - C06: `187420` cents (1,874.20 EUR)

### Verdict
**APPROVED** - Age calculation handles day-of-month boundaries correctly without syntax errors.
